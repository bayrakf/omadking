> Hey ProductHunt! 👋
> 
> As someone doing strict One Meal A Day (OMAD) while training heavily in the evening, I ran into a constant wall: **energy dips, wrong meal timing, and guesswork on post-workout recovery.**
> 
> Eating 2,500+ kcal in a single window while ensuring you don't crash during a 19:00 workout requires exact macro timing.
> 
> So I built **OMADCoach**:
> - ⚡ **AI Meal Timing Engine**: Calculates exact eating window & pre/post-workout meal timing based on your sport & intensity.
> - 🍲 **Meal Prep Reheat Guides**: Tells you step-by-step how to reheat yesterday’s prepped meal so it tastes fresh.
> - 📊 **Automatic Harris-Benedict Macro Split**: Tailored protein, carb, and fat targets for performance or weight loss.
> - 💬 **AI Nutritionist Coach**: Answers instant Q&A on electrolytes, fasting windows, and recovery.

## 2. Reddit Posts (r/OMAD & r/intermittentfasting)

**Title:** Built an app to solve the OMAD + evening workout energy crash (AI macro timing & reheat prep guides)

### Post Body
> Hey everyone,
> 
> I’ve been doing OMAD for a while and training (running + heavy lifting) around 18:00–19:00. The biggest struggle was always energy timing: *When do I eat so I don't feel sluggish during workouts or starve post-workout?*
> 
> To solve this, I built **OMADCoach** (React Native + Supabase + Gemini AI):
> 
> 1. Input your workout (sport, duration, intensity, start time).
> 2. It calculates your TDEE & optimal eating window around that specific workout.
> 3. AI generates a full meal recipe + **reheat instructions** for meal prep from the day before.
> 
> It's free to try (3 plans/week on free tier).
